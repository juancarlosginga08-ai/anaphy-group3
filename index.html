Main organs

The 4 key organs

Organ 1

Testes

Produce sperm and male hormones such as testosterone.

Organ 2

Epididymis

Stores and matures sperm before they are released.

Organ 3

Vas Deferens

Carries sperm from the epididymis toward the urethra.

Organ 4

Penis

Delivers sperm during reproduction and helps in urination.
